Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

LeadTracker is a tool for keeping track of sales leads from business cards and lead cards. This page explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • LeadTracker has no servers and no database of its own. We never receive your leads.
  • Your leads are stored in your own browser. If you sign in with Google, they're also saved to your own Google Drive.
  • Emails are only sent from your Gmail after you review the message and confirm the sender and recipient.
  • We don't sell, share or analyze your data, and the app has no ads or tracking.

Information you put into the app

You may add contact details from cards (names, companies, job titles, email addresses, phone numbers and websites), card photos, notes and meeting dates. This information is stored in your browser on your device. You can delete any lead at any time, and clearing your browser's site data removes all of it from that device.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

If you choose to sign in with Google, LeadTracker receives your name, email address and profile picture so it can show who is signed in. With your permission, it saves your leads to your Google Drive so they stay in sync across your devices.

LeadTracker asks only for access to files it creates itself. It can't see, read or change any other files in your Google Drive. This data goes straight from your browser to Google and is never sent to us. LeadTracker's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove LeadTracker's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sending emails from Gmail

If you choose to send a follow-up email from LeadTracker, the app asks Google for permission to send email from your Gmail account. It uses that permission only to send the specific email you review and confirm, to the address you confirm. LeadTracker cannot read, search or delete your email. The message goes straight from your browser to Gmail, and a copy appears in your Gmail “Sent” folder as usual. You can remove this permission at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Card scanning and the AI assistant

Card photos are read on your own device. The optional AI assistant also runs on your device, using either your browser's built-in AI model or a model that LeadTracker downloads once. Your leads, photos and questions are not sent to an AI service.

To do this, the app downloads code and AI model files from public services: jsDelivr and esm.run for software libraries, and Hugging Face for AI model files. Like any website, these services can see basic technical information such as your IP address when your browser downloads from them. They do not receive your leads.

Hosting

LeadTracker is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic technical information, such as IP addresses, when the site is visited. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Other people's information

The contact details you scan belong to other people. Please use them only for legitimate business follow-up and in line with the laws that apply to you.

Children

LeadTracker is a business tool and is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
